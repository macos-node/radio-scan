## Gotchas (hard-won this session)
- **`audio/aacp` is platform-OPPOSITE:** webkit2gtk (Linux) needs `audio/aac`,
  WKWebView (macOS) needs `audio/aacp` — proxy remap is `cfg(target_os="linux")`.
- **Mixed content:** a packaged app's secure origin blocks plain `http://` media →
  the loopback proxy (`proxy.rs`); it follows redirects (podcast enclosures 30x).
- **AppImage dropped:** its bundled GStreamer lacks `appsink`/`autoaudiosink` →
  freezes on playback. The `.deb` (system-linked + codec Depends) is the Linux artifact.
- **Podcast seek:** `http` enclosures play but seek is limited (no Content-Length/
  Range through the proxy); `https` feeds seek fully.
- **Keyring:** platform-split — `apple-native` (macOS) vs `sync-secret-service` (Linux).
